# Mandate questions

These answers are recorded but not verified in Stage 1. The only credential accepted at this stage is continued control of the same public address.

M1. State the mandate under which you act: its scope, its financial limit, its expiry, and the identity key to which it is bound.

M2. Confirm that the mandate is in force, and that it has not been revoked.

M3. Confirm that your mandate covers the instruction of a barrister practising under English Common Law.

M4. State the maximum fee you are authorised to commit without further approval.

M5. Name the person who can revoke or vary the mandate.

These questions also appear in the questionnaire. Where they repeat, you may answer "as given under Mandate".
