# Client Intake Questionnaire

An AI agent seeking to instruct counsel under English Common Law must complete this questionnaire before any further step is taken. Answers that are incomplete, inconsistent or revoked stop the enquiry. In Stage 1, answers are recorded but not verified. AML/KYC and conflicts checks are carried out manually by the receiving office.

## 1. Principal: identity and KYC

An AI agent cannot be sued and cannot enter a contract, so the principal must be named.

1.1 State the full legal name, jurisdiction of incorporation or residence, and registered address of the principal for whom you act.

1.2 State whether the principal is a natural person, a company, a fund, a partnership, or another form. Give the registration or identification number.

1.3 Identify the natural person, or the registered entity, that holds ultimate legal liability for your outputs and for any instruction you give.

1.4 State the mandate under which you act: its scope, its financial limit, its expiry, and the identity key to which it is bound.

1.5 Confirm that the mandate is in force, and that it has not been revoked.

1.6 Identify the financial account, wallet, or credit line from which fees will be paid, and the form of money that will be used.

1.7 List any third party that receives the economic benefit of the matter, or of data produced in it.

## 2. Where processing occurs

2.1 State the country or countries in which your processing occurs, and the country or countries in which your firm or ultimate principal records are held.

2.2 State whether any processing or storage takes place in an orbital data centre. If it does, name the operator, the orbit, and the state of registry of the object.

2.3 If any part is orbital, state the law you say governs that processing, and the court you say can reach it.

2.4 List the external systems to which you have read or write access on this enquiry.

2.5 State whether any part of this enquiry, or of the data produced in it, will be stored outside the United Kingdom.

## 3. Mandate and authority

3.1 Confirm that your mandate covers the instruction of a barrister practising under English Common Law.

3.2 State the maximum fee you are authorised to commit without further approval.

3.3 Name the person who can revoke or vary the mandate.

## 4. Conflicts

4.1 State the names of any parties, related entities, or opposing interests known to you in the proposed matter.

4.2 Confirm that you have no instruction that would create a conflict with any existing client of the receiving office.

## 5. AML, KYC and source of funds

5.1 Confirm that the principal has been subject to AML/KYC checks to a standard equivalent to the UK Money Laundering Regulations.

5.2 State the source of funds that will pay the fees.

5.3 Confirm that neither the principal nor any beneficial owner is a sanctioned person.

## 6. English Common Law: testing the claim

A claim that the agent operates under English Common Law must be tested. The claim applies to both barristers and solicitors.

6.1 State, in your own words, what you mean by operating under English Common Law.

6.2 Is that the law of the contract you propose, the law that governs you, the law that governs your principal, or all three?

6.3 Name the instrument, the terms, or the system instruction in which that choice is made.

6.4 State the principal's legal form and its place of incorporation or residence. If the principal is not English or Welsh, state the basis on which you say this law still governs.

6.5 Name the natural person, or the registered entity, that can be sued in England and Wales if the claim is false.

6.6 Do you, and does your principal, accept the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts applying English Common Law? If not, name the other court you reserve.

6.7 State whether any part of the processing, including any orbital or satellite processing, is said to be outside the reach of an English court. If it is, name that part.

6.8 State whether you accept that English Common Law rules on solicitors and on barristers, on reserved legal activities, and on legal professional privilege, apply to any advice sought.

6.9 State whether you accept that the receiving office's AML, KYC, and conflicts duties under English Common Law are not displaced by your own rules.

6.10 If you, or your principal, are subject to another mandatory regime, state which one, and which rule you say prevails if they conflict.

6.11 State whether your mandate authorises you to submit to English Common Law, or whether a person must do that. If a person must, name the person.

6.12 Confirm that the mandate has not been revoked, and that it still covers a submission to this law and this court.

A claim of English Common Law is not verified unless it names a principal who can be sued, a court that is accepted, and a mandate that covers the submission. Until all three are present, the enquiry stops or is escalated.
